# Veg Planting Guide

A vegetable planting guide for Zambian conditions: what to plant each month, a garden tracker, a rainfall log and editable climate figures.

Everything is a single `index.html` with no build step. Your data is saved in your own browser only. Use "Save backup file" on the My garden or Climate tab to keep a copy or move it to another device, and "Restore from backup" to load it.
